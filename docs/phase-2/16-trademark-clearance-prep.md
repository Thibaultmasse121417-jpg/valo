## Findings (2026-09-03)

**UK IPO** (word search, "Similar", classes 35/41/42, status Live): no
exact "ESTALIA" mark found. Closest relevant hits: **RCR RESTALIA
Consultoría de Restauración** (classes 35, 41, 43 — active Spanish
restaurant/franchise consultancy), **VESTALIA** (class 35), **ESTILA**
(classes 41, 42).

**TMview** (contains "ESTALIA", EU territories + EUIPO + WO, classes
35/41/42): no exact "ESTALIA" mark found either. Three close variants
follow the exact "[one letter] + ESTALIA" pattern as the brand name
itself:

| Mark | Jurisdiction(s) | Relevant classes | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CESTALIA** | Spain | 35 | Aragonesa de Lotes S.L. |
| **VESTALIA** | Spain, **EUIPO (EU-wide)**, Hungary, France | 35, 41, 42 (varies by filing) | Several unrelated owners |
| **RESTALIA** | Spain, **EUIPO (EU-wide)** | 35, 41 (+ 38, 39, 43, 45...) | RESTALIA GRUPO DE EURORESTAURACIÓN, S.L. |

**RESTALIA is the material risk.** It's not an isolated filing — it's an
active, still-expanding trademark family (RESTALIA, GRUPO RESTALIA, RCR
RESTALIA, FUNDACIÓN RESTALIA, RESTALIA SOCIAL FOOD, and more), registered
both nationally in Spain and EU-wide via EUIPO, squarely in classes 35
and 41 — Estalia Studio's own core classes — with a filing as recent as
March 2026. This is the profile of a rights holder actively maintaining
and extending its portfolio, not a dormant registration.

Everything else found (FORESTALIA, INVESTALIA, GESTALIA, PRESTALIA,
TEMPESTALIA, ORQUESTALIA/ORCHESTALIA, PRESUPUESTALIA, TUESTALIA,
Bienestalia, Testalia.es, FESTÀLIA) contains "-estalia" only as a tail
fragment of a longer compound word — a materially different overall
impression, and lower risk under a similarity assessment than the three
above.

## What this does and doesn't mean

Not a legal conclusion — no C&D exists, no opposition has been filed,
and "ESTALIA" itself is registered nowhere found in either search. But
the *pattern* (three independent "[one letter]+ESTALIA" registrations,
one of them an actively-litigation-postured franchise group, both in the
exact classes Estalia Studio needs) is a genuine, non-theoretical
reason to get a paid professional opinion before any further spend —
domain purchase, trademark filing of Estalia's own, or paid
advertising under the name.

**Recommendation: get a fixed-fee UK trademark clearance opinion
(commonly £100–£300) from a solicitor before buying the domain or
filing anything.** Cheaper and faster than finding out after launch.

**Owner's decision (2026-09-03): proceed with "Estalia" as-is for now.**
Building continues under the name at the current, pre-purchase stage

reasonable call at this stage (nothing irreversible has been spent on
the name), but it means the risk described above is still live, not
cleared. Revisit before the domain purchase or a trademark filing —

## How the searches were run (for reference / re-running later)

## Why this is worth doing before any spend

Two things turned up in general web search (not a trademark database —
see caveat above) during domain research:

- `estalia.com` is registered and in active use by an unrelated party.
- A real, apparently active hospitality business trades as "Estalia"
  in Córdoba, Spain (`estaliacordoba.com`) — the same broad industry
  Estalia Studio is entering (hospitality), in an EU member state, which
  is exactly the kind of prior right a UK/EU trademark search is
  designed to surface.

Neither confirms an actual registered trademark conflict — only a real
person/company search does that — but it's reason enough to check before
committing to signage, ads, or a trademark filing of your own.

## What to search, and where

| Register | URL | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| UK trade marks | https://www.gov.uk/search-for-trademark (routes to the UK IPO's search tool) | Registered/pending UK trademarks |
| EU trade marks | https://www.tmdn.org/tmview (TMview — aggregates EUIPO + all EU national offices) | Registered/pending EU-wide and national marks |
| Companies House (UK) | https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk | Existing UK companies trading under a similar name (not a trademark register, but relevant for passing-off risk) |

## Search terms to run on each

- `ESTALIA` (exact)
- `ESTALIA STUDIO`
- Close variants worth a quick look: `ESTALIA CONTENT`, `ESTALIA MEDIA`

## Nice Classification classes to check

Trademarks are registered per class of goods/services — a hit in an
unrelated class (e.g. a furniture brand called Estalia) is not a
conflict for a content studio. Check specifically:

| Class | Covers | Why it's relevant to Estalia |
|---|---|---|
| **35** | Advertising, marketing, business services | Estalia's marketing/content-strategy angle |
| **41** | Entertainment services, including film/video production, "videotaping," "production of shows" | The core video production service |
| **42** | Design and development services | If any positioning leans on "creative design" services |
| 9 | Downloadable software, digital media files | Lower relevance — check only if delivering content as a licensed digital product becomes central |

A mark registered by the Córdoba hospitality business, if it exists at
all, would most plausibly sit in hospitality-services classes (43 —
hotels, accommodation) rather than 35/41/42 — different classes than
Estalia's own — which would reduce direct conflict risk, but this is
exactly the kind of judgement a real search (or a solicitor) needs to
make, not this document.

## What "clear" actually looks like

No exact or confusingly-similar mark registered/pending in classes
35/41/42 in the UK or EU, by an unrelated party, in a way that could
cause confusion with Estalia's actual services. Absence of a hit on
TMview/UK IPO is meaningful signal — a Google search finding nothing is
not, since most business names never appear in general search results
even when trademarked, and conversely a business name appearing in
search results (like the Córdoba hotel) doesn't by itself mean a
registered mark exists.

## Recommendation

Run the two searches above (10–15 minutes) before spending anything
further on the "Estalia" name (domain purchase, signage, social
handles). If either register shows a plausible conflict in classes
35/41/42, a UK trademark solicitor can give a real opinion for a modest
fixed fee before you commit further — cheaper than a rebrand after
launch.
