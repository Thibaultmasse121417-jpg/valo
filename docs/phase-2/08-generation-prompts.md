# Generation prompts

Ready to paste into Higgsfield (or an equivalent image/video model) the
moment credits are available. Two passes per demo: a fictional **source
still** first, then a **video transformation** of that exact still —
never generate video directly from text, so the "before" image stays a
real, reusable, Estalia-owned asset in its own right (it doubles as the
Immediate Proof "before" side).

General rules for every prompt below (do not repeat per-prompt, but every
generation must follow them):

- Photoreal, editorial hospitality photography style — not CGI, not
  illustration, not an obvious render.
- No people in source stills (avoids likeness/rights questions and dates
  the image less).
- No visible signage, brand names, or logos anywhere in frame.
- Natural or golden-hour light, no flash-lit or clinical lighting.
- 16:9 for the still (matches the poster/before use); the video pass
  should preserve the still's geometry exactly — same walls, windows,
  furniture, proportions, materials — moving the camera, not the room.

## 01 — Bedroom (Cinematic Hero)

**Still prompt:** "Editorial photograph of a boutique hotel bedroom,
independent luxury property, warm neutral palette, linen bedding, brass
and oak details, large window with soft natural sidelight, tasteful
minimal styling, no people, no visible branding, shot on a full-frame
camera at eye level, shallow depth of field, magazine-quality
composition, 16:9."

**Video prompt (from the still):** "Slow, single continuous cinematic
push forward into the room, very subtle, editorial pace, no cuts, no
camera shake, preserve the exact room geometry, furniture and lighting
of the source image, natural motion of light linen fabric only, 6–8
seconds, 24fps cinematic motion."

## 02 — Exterior (Architectural Movement)

**Still prompt:** "Editorial architectural photograph of an independent
boutique hotel façade, warm stone or render exterior, early evening
golden hour light, restrained landscaping, no people, no cars, no
visible signage, symmetrical or near-symmetrical composition, shot at
a natural human eye-level or slight low angle, 16:9."

**Video prompt:** "Slow lateral architectural drift across the façade,
very subtle parallax, tripod-smooth motion, no zoom, no crash push,
preserve the exact building geometry, windows and materials of the
source image, gentle movement of foliage or light only, 6–8 seconds."

## 03 — Pool & Resort (Social Reel)

**Still prompt:** "Editorial photograph of a boutique hotel pool deck,
minimal luxury sun loungers, still turquoise water, warm midday or
golden-hour light, restrained planting, no people, no branding, shot at
a low, immersive angle close to water level, 16:9."

**Video prompt:** "Gentle water ripple and light shimmer across the pool
surface, slow lateral or slight rising camera movement, aspirational
social pace — a touch faster and more dynamic than the bedroom or
exterior clips, but still smooth and editorial, not shaky handheld,
preserve exact deck and architecture geometry from the source image,
6–8 seconds."

## 04 — Restaurant (Editorial Dining)

**Still prompt:** "Editorial photograph of a boutique hotel restaurant
interior, one beautifully set table in soft focus foreground, warm
ambient evening light, tasteful minimal tableware, no people, no
branding, shallow depth of field, intimate close composition, 16:9."

**Video prompt:** "Slow, close, tactile camera drift across the table
setting — cutlery, glassware, natural light shift only — editorial
dining-content pace, no cuts, no dramatic movement, preserve the exact
table, room geometry and materials of the source image, 6–8 seconds."

## Negative / avoid list (apply to every generation)

Invented furniture or objects not in the source, moving or morphing
walls, duplicated objects, warped or curved windows, unrealistic
reflections, excessive parallax or wide-angle distortion, a CGI or
video-game look, unrealistic or uncanny people, random decorative
elements not present in the source, visible text or logos, watermarks.
